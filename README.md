# My favorite series :> 
**By: Angel L. Taneo**

1. **From (the series)**
*From follows strangers trapped in a sinister small town they cannot escape, besieged each night by deadly forest creatures as they search for the town’s secrets and a way out. *
2. **That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime**
*That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime follows a corporate worker who, after a fatal stabbing, awakens in a fantasy world as a slime with the power to absorb abilities, embarking on a mission to unite diverse races.*
